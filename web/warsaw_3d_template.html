Warsaw, floor by floor

Every coloured slab is one storey, coloured by the median price per m² of the flats sold on it (RCN notarial deeds, 2006–2026). By default each sale is re-priced to 2025 złoty with a quality-adjusted index, so a flat sold in 2012 and one sold last month are comparable. Dark massing = no flat sales recorded on that storey.
